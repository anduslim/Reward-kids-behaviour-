Positive reinforcement, gamified

Turn good habits into shiny stars.

A playful way to encourage everyday good behaviours in young kids — earn stars, build streaks, and trade them for wishlist rewards. Runs entirely in your browser.

Everything you need to cheer them on

Simple for kids, controlled by grown-ups.

🧒

Kid profiles & avatars

Add each child and build a fun, Kahoot-style avatar — hair, eyes, colours and more.

⭐

Stars for behaviours

Reward brushing teeth, tidying toys, sleeping early and more — down to half a star.

🎁

Wishlist rewards

Create rewards with a photo, cost and quantity. Kids redeem stars for treats.

🔥

Streaks & achievements

Daily streaks and milestone badges keep the motivation going, Reddit-style.

🔒

Parent PIN

A PIN protects grown-up actions like awarding stars and redeeming rewards.

💾

Private & portable

Everything is stored locally. Export a backup any time to move between devices.

🛠️

Make it your own

Star Kids is fully open source. Fork it on GitHub and open your copy in Claude Code to tweak behaviours, rewards, colours — anything — with plain-English prompts. No build tools to learn first.
